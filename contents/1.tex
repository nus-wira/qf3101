\section{Forward, Futures \& Hedging}
\subsection*{Formulas}
\[\sum_{i=0}^{n-1} ar^i = \frac{a(1-r^n)}{1-r}\]
For given rate $r\%$ over time period $t\in[0, T]$,
with annual period $P$,
discount factor $d_{0,T}$ is given
\[d_{0,T} = \left(1+r\% \times \frac{T}{P}\right)^{-1} < 1\]

\subsection*{No Arbitrage Argument}
To prove $A=B$, show strategies for $A\neq B$.
If $A>B$, short on $A$, long on $B$, and vice versa.

\subsection*{1.3.1 Marked-to-Market Mechanism}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Investor deposits intial margin
    \item Account is marked to market each day on 
    gain/loss of futures position
    \item Margin account balance flutuates day to day
    \item If balance drops below threshold, 
    investor required to top up with cash
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{1.3.2 Futures-Forwards Equivalence}
If interest rates are known to follow expectation dynamics,
then theoretical futures and forward prices of
corresponding contracts are identical.

\subsection*{1.4.1 Short Hedge}
To \textbf{lock in} a selling price, applicable when
\begin{itemize}
    \item already owns asset and expects to sell in futures
    \item does not own asset but will in future, 
    and sell later afterward
\end{itemize}
Net cash inflow of $F_0=S_T+(F_0-S_T)$ is locked in.\\
With basis risk,
\[F_1+b_2=S_2+(F_1-F_2)\]

\subsection*{1.4.2 Long Hedge}
To \textbf{lock in} a purchase price, applicable when hedger
has to purchase asset in the future.\\
Net cash outflow of $F_0=S_T-(S_T-F_0)$ is locked in.\\
With basis risk,
\[F_1+b_2=S_2-(F_2-F_1)\]

\subsection*{1.4.3 Basis Risk}
Perfect hedges often not possible in reality. 
\begin{enumerate}
    \item unavailability of futures contract 
    matching asset obligation dates
    \item quantity of asset obligated may not be 
    integer multiple of contract size
    \item lack of liquidity in futures market
    \item terms of delivery may not coincide with obligation
    \item unavailability of futures contracts 
    with underlying asset exactly matching asset hedged
\end{enumerate}
Define basis $b_i$ at time $t_i$, 
with spot price $S_i$ and futures price $F_i$ as
\[b_i=S_i-F_i\]
\begin{center}
\includegraphics[width=0.5\linewidth]{contents/1.4.3.png}
\end{center}
Effective price can be written as
\[F_1 + b_2 = S_1 - (b_1 - b_2)\]
Price at time $t_1$, offset by diff btw 
\textbf{initial basis} $b_1$ and \textbf{cover basis} $b_2$.\\
Should choose futures contract with maturity close,
but later than expiration of hedge.

\subsection*{1.4.4 Cross Hedging}
Futures and spot price of proxy asset at time $t_i$
denoted by $F_i^*$ and $S_i^*$ respectively.
Effective price achieved is
\[S_2+(F_1^* - F_2^*) = F_1^* + (S_2^* - F_2^*) + (S_2 - S_2^*)\]
